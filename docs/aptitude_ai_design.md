# Aptitude AI Design – Zecpath AI

## Objective
Evaluate candidate reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making skills during AI interviews.

## Core Components

### 1. Question Engine
- Logical reasoning questions
- Situational judgment scenarios
- Role-based cognitive questions

### 2. Answer Understanding Engine
- Extract reasoning steps
- Detect structured thinking
- Identify decision-making patterns

### 3. Scoring Engine
- Logical correctness
- Clarity of reasoning
- Completeness of approach
- Decision quality

### 4. Scenario Evaluator
- Compares candidate responses with ideal patterns
- Evaluates effectiveness of decisions

### 5. Output
- Aptitude Score (0–100)
- Reasoning Breakdown
- Strengths & Weaknesses

---

## Evaluation Flow

Question
↓
Candidate Answer
↓
Reasoning Analysis
↓
Scenario Evaluation
↓
Aptitude Scoring
↓
Final Report
