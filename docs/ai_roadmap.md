# Zecpath AI Future Roadmap (2026–2028)

## Objective

Define the future evolution of the Zecpath AI Recruitment Platform by introducing advanced AI capabilities, improving candidate evaluation, and preparing the system for enterprise-scale deployment.

---

# Phase 1 – Intelligence Enhancement (0–6 Months)

## Goals

- Integrate Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Improve answer understanding
- Enhance technical scoring
- Reduce scoring bias
- Improve behavioral signal analysis

### Features

- GPT/Llama-based answer evaluation
- Improved semantic matching
- Adaptive interview questioning
- Better confidence scoring

---

# Phase 2 – Advanced AI Features (6–12 Months)

## Goals

Introduce multimodal AI.

### Features

- AI Video Analysis
- Emotion Detection
- Real-time Interview Feedback
- Adaptive Follow-up Questions
- Voice Quality Analysis

---

# Phase 3 – Candidate Intelligence (12–18 Months)

## Goals

Provide personalized candidate guidance.

### Features

- AI Coaching System
- Candidate Improvement Suggestions
- Resume Enhancement Suggestions
- Personalized Learning Roadmap

---

# Phase 4 – Recruiter Intelligence (18–24 Months)

## Goals

Improve recruiter productivity.

### Features

- Interview Analytics Dashboard
- Hiring Funnel Analytics
- Candidate Benchmarking
- Team Performance Reports

---

# Phase 5 – Enterprise AI (24+ Months)

## Goals

Build a scalable enterprise hiring platform.

### Features

- Multi-language Interviews
- Company-specific Interview Models
- Continuous AI Learning
- Cloud-native Deployment
- Multi-agent AI Architecture

---

## Expected Benefits

- Better hiring quality
- Faster interviews
- Reduced recruiter workload
- Improved candidate experience
- Enterprise scalability
