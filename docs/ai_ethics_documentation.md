# Zecpath AI Ethics Framework

## Objective

To ensure the HR AI system operates in a fair, transparent, and responsible manner while protecting candidate rights and personal data.

---

# Core Ethical Principles

## Fairness

- No discrimination based on gender, age, religion, caste, ethnicity, or location.
- Candidate evaluation is based only on job-relevant signals.
- Equal scoring logic applied across all applicants.

## Transparency

- AI decisions must be explainable.
- Recruiters can review score breakdowns.
- Decision factors are visible and auditable.

## Accountability

- Every AI decision is traceable.
- Logs are maintained for audit purposes.
- Human review is available when required.

## Privacy

- Candidate information is securely stored.
- Sensitive data is masked before scoring.
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

---

# Candidate Consent Requirements

Before starting an AI interview:

Candidates must be informed that:

- AI will evaluate their responses.
- Their data will be stored and processed.
- Voice or audio may be recorded and converted into text.

Candidates must explicitly agree to:

- AI-based evaluation.
- Data processing.
- Storage of interview information.

---

# Data Handling Policy

| Data Type | Action |
|------------|---------|
| Resume | Stored securely |
| Audio | Converted to text |
| Personal Information | Masked before scoring |
| Interview Logs | Stored for audit purposes |
