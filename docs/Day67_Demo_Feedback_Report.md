## Mock Q&A

### Q1. How accurate is the AI system?

**Answer**

The AI system achieves approximately **88–92% accuracy** during internal testing.

---

### Q2. How is bias reduced?

**Answer**

Sensitive attributes are excluded, score normalization is applied, and fairness checks are performed throughout the hiring pipeline.

---

### Q3. Can the system scale?

**Answer**

Yes. The architecture uses independent AI services with horizontal scaling support.

---

### Q4. How is this different from a traditional ATS?

**Answer**

Traditional ATS systems only screen resumes. Zecpath AI automates resume screening, interviews, behavioral analysis, technical evaluation, and hiring decisions.
