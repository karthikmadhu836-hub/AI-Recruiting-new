# Day 69 – AI Internship Portfolio

## Title

**Zecpath AI Developer Internship Portfolio**

---

# Project Overview

The **Zecpath AI Hiring System** is an end-to-end AI-powered recruitment platform developed during a 70-day internship project. The system automates candidate evaluation from resume screening to final hiring decisions.

---

# Project Objectives

- Automate resume screening using ATS
- Conduct AI-based screening interviews
- Evaluate HR and technical interview performance
- Analyze behavioral and integrity signals
- Generate explainable hiring decisions
- Provide recruiter-friendly reports

---
