1. Quality Control Analyst
Job Summary
The Quality Control Analyst is responsible for performing laboratory testing of raw
materials, in-process samples, and finished products to ensure compliance with quality
standards and regulatory guidelines.
Key Responsibilities
• Perform chemical and/or microbiological testing as per SOPs
• Analyze raw materials, intermediates, and finished products
• Operate analytical instruments (HPLC, GC, UV, FTIR)
• Record and review test data accurately
• Follow Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and Good Laboratory Practices (GLP)
• Report deviations, out-of-specification (OOS) results
• Assist in method validation and stability studies
Qualifications
• B.Sc / M.Sc in Chemistry, Microbiology, Biotechnology, or related field
Skills Required
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
• Knowledge of laboratory instruments
• Attention to detail
• Basic understanding of GMP/GLP
