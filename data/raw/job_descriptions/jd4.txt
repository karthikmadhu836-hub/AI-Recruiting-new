4. QC Chemist
Job Summary
The QC Chemist performs chemical analysis of substances to ensure product quality and
safety.
Key Responsibilities
• Conduct chemical testing and analysis
• Operate instruments like HPLC, GC, UV
• Prepare reagents and standards
• Maintain lab documentation
• Ensure compliance with testing protocols
Qualifications
• B.Sc / M.Sc in Chemistry
Skills Required
• Strong chemistry fundamentals
• Instrument handling
• Data interpretation
