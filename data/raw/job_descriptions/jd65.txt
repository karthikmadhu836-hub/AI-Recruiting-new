65. Quality Compliance Freelancer
Job Summary
A Quality Compliance Freelancer works independently on short-term projects related to
audits, documentation, regulatory compliance, and quality system improvements.
Key Responsibilities
• Prepare and review SOPs, quality manuals, and validation documents
• Assist companies in GMP/ISO compliance projects
• Conduct gap analysis of quality systems
• Support regulatory submissions and inspections
• Provide remote consultation for quality issues
• Help implement CAPA and deviation management systems
• Assist startups in setting up QC/QA systems
Qualifications
• B.Sc / M.Sc / B.Pharm / M.Pharm / Engineering background
• Strong industry experience preferred (3–10+ years)
Skills Required
• Strong documentation and regulatory knowledge
• Independent working ability
• Analytical and problem-solving mindset
• Communication and consulting skills
