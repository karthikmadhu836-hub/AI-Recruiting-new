30. QC Documentation Specialist
Job Summary
The QC Documentation Specialist manages and controls all quality control documents to
ensure accuracy, traceability, and compliance with regulatory standards.
Key Responsibilities
• Prepare, review, and maintain QC documents (SOPs, STPs, reports)
• Ensure proper documentation practices (GDP – Good Documentation Practices)
• Control document lifecycle (creation, revision, archival, retrieval)
• Maintain logbooks, registers, and documentation systems
• Ensure documents comply with GMP and regulatory requirements
• Support audits by providing required documentation
Qualifications
• B.Sc / M.Sc / B.Pharm or related field
Skills Required
• Strong documentation and writing skills
• Knowledge of GMP and GDP
• Attention to detail
• Organizational skills
