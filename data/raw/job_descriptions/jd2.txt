2. Junior QC Analyst
Job Summary
The Junior QC Analyst supports senior analysts in laboratory testing, documentation, and
routine quality checks.
Key Responsibilities
• Assist in sample preparation and testing
• Maintain laboratory records and logbooks
• Calibrate instruments under supervision
• Follow SOPs and safety guidelines
• Support stability testing and documentation
Qualifications
• B.Sc in Science (Chemistry/Biology preferred)
Skills Required
• Basic lab knowledge
• Willingness to learn
• Teamwork and discipline
