20. Stability Study Scientist
Job Summary
The Stability Study Scientist designs and manages stability programs to determine product
shelf-life under various environmental conditions.
Key Responsibilities
• Design stability study protocols (accelerated, long-term, intermediate)
• Store samples in stability chambers (ICH conditions)
• Analyze samples at defined intervals
• Trend and interpret stability data
• Prepare stability reports for regulatory submissions
• Coordinate with QA and regulatory teams
Qualifications
• M.Sc / B.Pharm / M.Pharm or related field
Skills Required
• Knowledge of ICH stability guidelines (Q1A)
• Data analysis and reporting
• Attention to detail
