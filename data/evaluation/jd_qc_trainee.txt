8. Trainee – Quality Control
Job Summary
The QC Trainee undergoes structured training in laboratory testing, documentation, and
quality systems.
Key Responsibilities
• Learn laboratory techniques and SOPs
• Assist in testing under supervision
• Maintain training records
• Follow safety and compliance guidelines
Qualifications
• B.Sc / M.Sc (Freshers)
Skills Required
• Learning attitude
• Discipline and adaptability
• Basic scientific knowledge
